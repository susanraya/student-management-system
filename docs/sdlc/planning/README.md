### EPIC-001 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-001

Single business owner can create and access their own account via email and password; no multi-user roles, employee workflows, or billing.

Covers owner sign-in, sign-out/session expiry, and free-of-charge use as a single-account personal tool.

**Related:** US-001, US-002, US-003

### EPIC-002 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-002

Owner manually records text-only expense entries (amount, date, category, note) and can view, edit, delete, filter and sort them.

Expenses are text records only, no receipt/PDF attachments; tracks expenses only, not income/revenue/profit; automatic bank/card import is out of scope.

**Related:** US-004, US-005, US-006, US-007, US-008

### EPIC-003 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-003

App ships with common business expense categories and lets the owner add/edit custom categories.

Every expense belongs to a single category; no category budgets or overspending alerts are included.

**Related:** US-009, US-010

### EPIC-004 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-004

Primary output: an on-screen dashboard with summaries and charts of total spending by category and by month.

Summaries reflect expenses only -- no income, profit, or budgets.

**Related:** US-011, US-012

### EPIC-005 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-005

Owner can select a single app currency in settings, defaulting to USD.

No multi-currency support and no live exchange rates.

**Related:** US-013

### EPIC-006 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-006

Secondary convenience feature letting the owner export expense records as a CSV file to share with an accountant.

Summaries and charts remain the primary output; CSV export is secondary.

**Related:** US-014
